OpenZH // About

About OpenZH

OpenZH is a browser port of the 2003 real-time strategy game engine. The engine is compiled from its GPL-3.0 source release to WebAssembly and draws through WebGPU, so a match needs no install and no plugin.

Your files stay yours

You select the game data ZIP files once. They are kept in this browser's private storage and are never uploaded, copied to a server or included in this project.

A port in progress

Skirmish against the computer and the campaign are the first goals, and both still have rough edges. The issue tracker is where the known problems and the work in progress live.

Independent and unofficial

OpenZH is an unofficial fan project, not affiliated with or endorsed by Electronic Arts. The game data belongs to its owners; you supply your own copy.

Support OpenZH

OpenZH is free and stays free. A tip is optional, unlocks nothing and only helps cover the time and the hosting behind the port.

Have a suggestion, a bug report or a feature idea? Send it in the PayPal message when you tip, together with the page and the version if you can. You can also open an issue and keep the conversation public.
